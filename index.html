The house

An exclusive dance performance club

King's Elite Lounge is not a bar with a playlist. It is a stage. Every night, exceptionally talented performers from India, Pakistan and Nepal take the floor, blending global tunes with the vibrant heart of Bollywood.

A state-of-the-art stage, a crystal-clear sound system and dazzling lighting turn Bur Dubai's creekside into the loudest, most luxurious room in the city. Unparalleled entertainment, genuine luxury and non-stop energy — from the first beat at 9:30 PM to the last at 4:00 AM.

  • Daily live shows with a rotating cast of performers
  • Bollywood, Punjabi and international sets, back to back
  • Hosted VIP tables with dedicated service throughout the night
  • Ground floor of Concorde Creek View Hotel, minutes from Al Fahidi Metro
